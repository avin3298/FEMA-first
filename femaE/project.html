Our Story

Our Story Launched with love on March 11, 2025, FemEase is my heartfelt gift to every beautiful woman. I’m Priyanka Kanojiya, and I created FemEase to support women’s wellness with natural, science-backed care. It’s more than a product — it’s a mission to spread menstrual health awareness, especially in rural areas, where it’s needed most. Crafted by a woman, for women — For your strength. Your cycle. Your shine..

Today, we're proud to offer a range of products developed by women, for women. Each formula is carefully crafted with natural ingredients and backed by research to provide real solutions for menstrual comfort, hormonal balance, and overall wellbeing.
